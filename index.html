FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

Pricing, licensing, emergencies, and the water itself, covered below.

Are you licensed for work in Lucan and Biddulph?

Yes, licensed and filing permits through the proper authority for any drain, vent, or supply work. Rural township or village, the standard holds.

Can you come urgently out this way?

We run priority response for bursts, backups, and no-water calls across Lucan, Parkhill, Ailsa Craig, and the gravel between them.

Water heater in a cold outbuilding, is that a problem?

It is solvable. Insulation, heat trace, and sometimes relocation keep the unit and its piping alive through north Middlesex January cold.

Old house with patchy repairs. Full repipe or more patches?

When clamps outnumber original fittings, the piping is done. Repiping in stages keeps cost manageable, and pressure comes back like a new house.

Do you handle septic-side plumbing?

Yes, drain runs to the tank, bed-friendly cleaning methods, and the fixtures that feed it are all regular work on Biddulph properties.

Our well stains and smells. Where do we start?

With a test, always. The lab sheet drives the treatment order, iron first, softener second, UV where bacteria show up after flooding.

How do you handle pricing?

Straight quotes in writing before work starts, with repair and replacement options priced side by side when both are honest answers.

How far from Lucan do you go?

Across Biddulph and neighboring townships, taking in Parkhill, Ailsa Craig, Granton, Denfield, Exeter, and down into London.

Do you serve areas outside of Lucan, Ontario?

Yes. We serve Lucan and nearby communities including Parkhill, Ailsa Craig, Granton, Denfield, and the surrounding area.
